Is the App Ready for Tradies to Switch?
Honestly — the web app is ready. The mobile story is not.
The web onboarding is genuinely best-in-class. A tradie can sign up and create their first job in under 3 minutes — faster than any competitor. The UI is cleaner than Tradify, the feature set is more complete than ServiceM8, and the AI Receptionist is a feature no competitor has.
But tradies live on their phones. The App Store and Google Play links in your codebase both return 404. Until those apps are published and searchable, you will lose every trial that starts on a phone. That is the single biggest thing standing between you and $10K MRR.
Pricing Recommendation
Your current pricing ($39 Pro / $49+$29/seat Team) is too cheap for what you are offering. You are giving away AI features and SWMS compliance at a price that signals "beta project" rather than "professional tool."
Recommended post-beta pricing:
Pro: $59/mo (still $3/mo cheaper than Tradify's cheapest plan per user)
Team: $79/mo base + $35/seat
AI Receptionist: Keep at $60/mo, but add usage-based billing above 100 calls/mo
A 5-person team on JobRunner at the recommended pricing would pay $219/mo — still $91/mo cheaper than Tradify and $176/mo cheaper than ServiceM8, while offering more features than both.
