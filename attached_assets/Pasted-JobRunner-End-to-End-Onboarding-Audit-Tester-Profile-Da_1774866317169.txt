JobRunner End-to-End Onboarding
Audit
Tester Profile: Dave Mitchell, Plumber, Cairns (2 workers) Date: March 30, 2026
Executive Summary
Overall Onboarding Rating: 8.5 / 10
The JobRunner onboarding experience is genuinely one of the best in the trade
software market. The transition from “Beta” to “Early Access” and the addition of the
“Try the Demo” button on the homepage are massive improvements. The “Life of a
Job” walkthrough and the pre-seeded demo data (which intelligently adapts to the
selected trade) provide immediate value without requiring the user to do any heavy
lifting.
However, there are still a few friction points and edge cases that need tightening
before a full public launch.
The Top 5 Things to Fix Immediately
1. Email Verification Hard Block: If a user signs up but misses the verification
email, they are completely blocked from logging in, with a generic error message.
There is no “Resend Verification Email” button on the login failure screen.
2. Missing Loading States: When navigating between complex views (like Insights
or Team Management), the app shows a generic, unbranded browser-level
loading spinner on a white screen. This feels unpolished compared to the rest of
the app.
3. Duplicate Email Error UI: When trying to sign up with an existing email, the API
returns an error, but the UI needs to clearly guide the user to the Login page or
offer a Password Reset option right there.
4. “What You Missed” Popup: This popup appears immediately on the demo
dashboard. While the feature is cool, it blocks the user’s first view of the
dashboard. It should be delayed or minimized for first-time users.
5. Mobile App Store Links: While the web onboarding is excellent, the App Store
and Google Play links are still returning 404s. A tradie who tries to download the
app immediately after signing up will hit a dead end.
Dealbreakers (What would make a tradie give up)
The Verification Wall: Tradies often sign up on their phone while on site, but
their work email might be on their office computer. If they can’t verify
immediately, they will close the app. Allowing a 24-hour grace period before
forcing email verification would significantly increase conversion.

Part 2 — Edge Cases & Error Handling
Step 7: Duplicate Email Sign Up
Status: FAIL
Copy: {"error": "User with this email already exists"} (API Response)
What Dave thinks: “I think I signed up for this a month ago… how do I log in?”
Friction: The UI needs to catch this error and gracefully say: “Looks like you
already have an account! [Click here to log in] or [Reset Password]”.
Step 8: Unverified Email Login Attempt
Status: FAIL
Copy: "Please verify your email address before logging in. Check your
email for verification instructions."
What Dave thinks: “I can’t find the email. Can you just send it again?”
Friction: There is no “Resend Email” button on the login screen. If the user lost
the email, they are permanently locked out.
Step 9: Loading States (Insights & Team Management)
Status: FAIL
Screenshots: step17_loading_state.webp , step21_insights_loading.webp
What Dave thinks: “Is my internet dropping out, or is the app broken?”
Friction: When navigating to heavy pages like Insights, the screen goes
completely white with a generic blue loading spinner. It breaks the illusion of a
snappy, native app. Skeleton loaders (gray boxes shaped like the UI) should be
used instead.
The Verdict
JobRunner is 90% of the way to a flawless onboarding experience. The core product
logic — specifically the trade-aware demo data — is brilliant. A plumber sees plumbing
jobs; an electrician sees electrical jobs. That personalization is what converts trials
into paying users.
To get to 100%, you need to fix the edge cases. Tradies are impatient. If they hit a white
loading screen, or get locked out because they can’t find a verification email, they will
bounce back to ServiceM8. Fix the verification flow, add skeleton loaders, and handle
duplicate emails gracefully, and you will have the highest-converting funnel in the
industry.
