SEQUENCE:
Step 1 — iPhone (Owner)
Open the dispatch board or job creation screen. Create a new job — client name, address, job type. Assign it to the worker account on Samsung. Tap confirm/send.
Step 2 — Samsung (Worker)
Push notification fires on Samsung instantly. Worker sees job assigned notification on lock screen or within app. Worker opens notification, sees full job details, taps Accept.
Step 3 — iPhone (Owner)
Owner can see worker has accepted. Job status updates in real time on dispatch board.
Step 4 — Samsung (Worker)
Worker taps Start Job or Start Timer. GPS tracking activates.
Step 5 — iPhone (Owner)
Owner opens GPS map view. Samsung worker’s live location dot appears on the map moving in real time.
Step 6 — Samsung (Worker)
Worker taps Complete Job.
Step 7 — iPhone (Owner)
Completed job notification fires on iPhone. Owner sees job flagged as complete and not yet invoiced. Owner taps Create Invoice — invoice auto-generates from time tracking data. Owner taps Send — client receives SMS payment link automatically.
Step 8 — iPhone (Owner)
Show the SMS firing to client. Briefly open the client payment portal link to show what the client sees. Client pays. Invoice status updates to Paid automatically. Receipt generated.
