2. Retell AI: Australian Number Verification
Retell AI does not offer native Australian phone numbers for purchase. However, it officially supports using Australian numbers from third-party providers like Twilio through a SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) trunk. [1]
Integration Method
The process involves:
Purchasing an Australian number from Twilio.
Creating an "Elastic SIP Trunk" within Twilio.
Configuring the trunk's "Termination" (for outbound calls) and "Origination" (for inbound calls) settings to point to Retell's servers.
Importing the number into the Retell AI dashboard. [2]
This method is technically sound but is more complex than a direct integration.
Identified Blockers
A critical blocker was identified in a Retell community forum post from March 2026. A user reported that all outbound calls to Australian numbers were failing with a SIP 403 error, indicating a permission issue. [3]
Error from Detail Logs:
Error dialing to user, SIP status code: 403 SIP error category: telephony_provider_permission_denied sip status: 403: Dialed number +61... is not included in whitelisted countries USA,CAN D13
This error occurred because of an account-level country whitelist that defaults to only the US and Canada. The user had to contact Retell support to manually add Australia to their account's whitelist. This represents a significant potential friction point and delay in the setup process, as it relies on manual intervention from the Retell team.
3. Vapi: Australian Number Verification
Vapi, like Retell, does not offer native Australian numbers. Their documentation explicitly states that free numbers are for US use only and that international numbers must be imported from Twilio. [4]
