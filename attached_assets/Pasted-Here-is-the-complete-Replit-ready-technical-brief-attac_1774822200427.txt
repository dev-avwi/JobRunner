The Big Answer on Twilio Numbers
Yes — tradies can buy a number straight through JobRunner, automatically. You do NOT need to manually buy numbers first. Here is exactly how it works:
Tradie clicks "Add AI Receptionist" and pays via Stripe.
Your backend receives the Stripe webhook (checkout.session.completed).
Your server calls the Twilio API to search for available Australian mobile numbers: GET /AvailablePhoneNumbers/AU/Local.json
It purchases the best available number: POST /IncomingPhoneNumbers.json
It immediately imports that number into Vapi.ai: POST /phone-number/import
The number is saved to that tradie's account in your database.
The tradie sees their new number appear on screen — the whole thing takes about 10 seconds.
The only thing to be aware of: Australian numbers may require a regulatory bundle (address verification) with Twilio. You need to pre-create one master regulatory bundle in your Twilio account that covers all your sub-numbers. This is a one-time setup, not per customer.
The Three Systems Explained
1. Website Add-on (Web + Mobile)
An iframe preview of their live site, their domain status, and a "Change Request" form where they drop notes on what needs updating. Those notes flow directly into your Admin Dashboard as tickets. No drag-and-drop editor — that would be a nightmare to build and maintain. The notes system is cleaner and more honest about what the service is.
2. AI Receptionist Add-on (Web + Mobile)
After the automated provisioning above, the tradie gets a simple config screen:
Their new number (e.g., +61 4XX XXX XXX)
"Forward calls to my mobile" toggle
"Business context" prompt textarea (what the AI says about their business)
Business hours picker
All of this maps directly to Vapi.ai's assistant configuration API. When they save changes, your backend calls PATCH /assistant/{id} on Vapi.
3. Admin Super-Dashboard (Your Internal Workflow)
This is the one that will transform your day-to-day:
Global Activity Feed: Real-time stream of every new trial, payment, error, and website change request across all businesses.
Shadow Mode / Impersonation: Click "Login as User" on any business account. Your backend generates a short-lived JWT with impersonator: true — you see exactly what they see, fix their AI prompt or debug their invoice, without ever touching their password. Every impersonation is logged in an audit table.
Website Request Kanban: A simple To Do / In Progress / Done board for managing change requests.
System Health Alerts: Failed Twilio provisioning, failed Stripe webhooks, and app errors all surface here.
