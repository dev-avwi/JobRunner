The 4 Things That Could Get You Sued — Fix These
1. Stripe Connect Boilerplate (Critical — financial liability)
Because tradies accept payments from homeowners through your platform, Stripe legally requires you to include specific text in your Terms binding each tradie to the Stripe Connected Account Agreement. Without it, if a tradie commits fraud through your platform, Stripe can hold you partially liable. The exact text to copy-paste into Section 10 of your Terms is in the PDF report.
2. Call Recording Consent (Critical — criminal liability)
Under the Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979, recording or transcribing a phone call without the caller's knowledge is a criminal offence in Australia. Your AI Receptionist (Vapi.ai) transcribes every call. Your Terms say callers will be told it is "an automated system" — but they must also be explicitly told the call is being recorded and transcribed. Add one sentence to Section 24A.
3. Homeowner Data Deletion Requests (Medium — privacy complaint risk)
If a homeowner whose details are stored in a tradie's JobRunner account emails you asking to delete their data, you cannot legally just delete it — that data belongs to the tradie's business account. You need a clause that says: "If you are a client of a business using JobRunner, contact that business directly." Without this, you are caught in the middle of privacy complaints that are not your responsibility.
4. Signup Page Consent Notice (Medium — APP 5 compliance)
Australian Privacy Principle 5 requires you to notify users at the moment of data collection. The signup form needs a small line above the submit button: "By creating an account you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy." Both words must be clickable links. This is also a mandatory requirement for App Store and Google Play approval.
