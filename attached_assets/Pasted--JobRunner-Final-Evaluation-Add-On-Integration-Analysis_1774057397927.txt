## 3. Add-On Integration Analysis

The proposed new features are not just viable; they are a natural and powerful extension of the existing platform.

### 3.1. AI Receptionist

**Concept:** An AI-powered service that answers incoming phone calls and SMS messages 24/7, books jobs, answers common questions, and qualifies leads directly within the JobRunner ecosystem.

**Market Precedent:** This is a rapidly growing and validated market. Companies like FieldPulse (Operator AI) [1], Reception Genie [2], and AgentZap [3] are already offering this as a high-value service specifically for trades.

**Integration Points:** The AI Receptionist would integrate seamlessly into the existing JobRunner architecture:

*   **Phone Number Provisioning:** A new section in **Settings > Integrations** would allow a business to provision a new virtual phone number or port their existing one. This number becomes the AI's primary contact point.
*   **Knowledge Base:** The AI would be trained on the business's data already inside JobRunner: services offered (from price list items), team member availability (from the **Schedule**), and business details (from **Settings**).
*   **Action Triggers:** When a customer calls/texts:
    *   "*Can you fix a leaking tap?*" → The AI creates a new **Job** with the description, tagged as "New Lead".
    *   "*Are you available tomorrow morning?*" → The AI checks the **Schedule** and offers available time slots.
    *   "*What's your ABN?*" → The AI pulls the ABN from the business's **Settings**.
*   **Output:** New jobs, client details, and conversation transcripts would appear directly in the **Action Center** and **Chat** modules, creating a perfect, closed-loop workflow.

**Monetization:** This is a high-margin, recurring revenue add-on. It can be priced per call/SMS (e.g., $1-2 per interaction) or as a monthly flat fee (e.g., **$99/month** for up to 100 interactions).

### 3.2. Custom Website Builder

**Concept:** A simple, template-driven website builder that allows a trade business to create a professional, branded website in minutes, with leads and bookings flowing directly into JobRunner.

**Market Precedent:** This is a proven strategy for increasing customer stickiness and capturing more value. Tradify's "Instant Website" feature is a direct and successful competitor [4]. The core insight is that most tradies either have no website or a very poor one, and they do not want the hassle of dealing with a separate web developer.

**Integration Points:** This feature leverages data the user has already provided:

*   **Content Generation:** The website is auto-populated with:
    *   **Services Offered:** From the user's price list in **Documents > Templates**.
    *   **About Us:** From the business details in **Settings**.
    *   **Contact Form:** A lead capture form that creates a new **Client** and a new **Job** (tagged as "Website Lead") in JobRunner.
    *   **Online Booking:** A widget that pulls availability directly from the **Schedule**.
*   **Hosting & Management:** A new **Website** tab would appear in the main navigation, allowing the user to pick a template, customize colors/logo (pulled from **Settings > Brand**), and publish to a `business-name.jobrunner.com.au` subdomain or a custom domain.

**Monetization:** This is a powerful retention tool and another recurring revenue stream. It can be offered as a premium feature on higher-tier plans or as a standalone add-on for **$29-$49/month**, including hosting.

JobRunner is more than just a job management app; it is a true operating system for a trade business. The existing platform is robust, feature-complete, and demonstrates a level of polish and strategic thinking that is rare in any software, let alone one built by a single developer.
