The 5 Things That Will Get You Rejected
1. Vague location permission strings (Apple's #1 rejection reason)
Your NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription must be specific. Do not write "We need your location." Write:
"JobRunner uses your location to show your position on the dispatch map and to track travel distance for timesheets. Your location is only shared with your team."
2. No in-app account deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1v)
Mandatory since 2022. Every app that allows account creation must have a "Delete Account" option inside the app itself. If you don't have this in Settings, add it before you submit — it is an automatic rejection without it.
3. Background location declaration not submitted (Google)
Google requires you to fill out a Permissions Declaration Form in the Play Console and submit a 30-second demo video showing background location being used meaningfully. Your app will be automatically rejected without this.
4. In-App Purchase for subscriptions (Apple Guideline 3.1.1)
If users can subscribe or pay inside the iOS app, Apple requires you to use their IAP system. You cannot link out to your website for payment from within the app. This is a critical one — plan your iOS subscription flow accordingly.
5. App not testable — no demo credentials provided
Reviewers need a fully-featured demo account. Always include demo@jobrunner.com.au / demo123 in your submission notes, or it will be rejected automatically.
The iOS Privacy Manifest (Mandatory Since 2024)
You must configure a PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy file via your app.json. This declares which sensitive APIs you use and why. Expo handles most of this automatically for its own SDK packages. The safest approach: submit a TestFlight build first — Apple will email you within minutes listing any missing declarations, and you can fix them before the real App Review submission.

Complete every item on this list before your first submission to either store.
 1. Finalize app.json: Ensure bundleIdentifier (iOS) and package (Android) are correct and unique. Set version and build numbers.
 2. Create App Store Connect & Google Play Developer Accounts: These can take a few days to get approved. Do this now.
