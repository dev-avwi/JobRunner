Hi App Review Team,

Thank you for the detailed feedback. We've addressed all three items below and submitted a new build (1.1.0, build 64) for review.

Re: Guideline 3 – Pricing confirmation

Yes, we confirm the In-App Purchase pricing for "Business Monthly" is intentionally {{AUD $199.99 / month — confirm or replace with the figure shown in App Store Connect}}. Our Paid Apps Agreement is in effect and all StoreKit products are configured.

Re: Guideline 2.1(b) – Subscription not registering after purchase

Thank you for flagging this. We reproduced the issue — the subscription-state refresh wasn't running immediately after a successful StoreKit transaction in some flows. The new build now:

Re-validates entitlements with our backend immediately on transactionUpdated.
Refreshes subscription status on app foreground and after every successful purchase, before navigating away from the paywall.
Adds retry/idempotency on the receipt-validation endpoint so a transient network blip during the receipt callback no longer leaves the user in an unsubscribed state.
We've re-tested in the StoreKit sandbox on iPadOS 26.4.1 (iPad Air 11-inch, M3) and the subscription is now recognised immediately, with no second prompt.

Re: Guideline 2.1 – Navigation feature location
