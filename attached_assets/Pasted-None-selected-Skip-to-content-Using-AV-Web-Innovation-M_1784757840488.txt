Your entitlement request for Tap to Pay on iPhone has been granted.


To enable this entitlement, please review Setting up the entitlement for Tap to Pay on iPhone. To ensure successful integration of Tap to Pay on iPhone please review the information below.


—————————————————


Human Interface Guidelines
Please review the Human Interface Guidelines for Tap to Pay on iPhone. To provide the best experience for your users, please make the best effort to follow these guidelines as closely as possible. If you are interested in feedback on your UX/UI implementation of Tap to Pay on iPhone, you may respond to this thread and we will reach out to assist.


—————————————————


App Store Connect Submission
When submitting your app to App Store Connect for the first time with the Tap to Pay on iPhone entitlement, you will need to provide additional details as part of your submission. You will need to add the following information in the notes section of your App Store Connect submission:
      - Declare how you are using the Tap to Pay on iPhone Entitlement (e.g. general availability, behind a feature flag, etc)
      - Add details on how the app store team can review your implementation of Tap to Pay on iPhone - you can do one or more of the following:
                - Provide a Test Account for App Store Team (recommended)
      - Provide a Video walkthrough or a Document (PDF) to highlight the following step-by-step processes:
                - Enrollment of Tap to Pay on iPhone, accepting terms and conditions.
                - Add items to cart, initiate transaction with Tap to Pay on iPhone
      - Successfully completed transaction

Note: Tap to Pay on iPhone transaction screens cannot be recorded with screen recording tools. Please use a second device to capture video of the iPhone completing the tasks above.


Note: If the app store team requires additional details on your implementation of Tap to Pay on iPhone - this will be presented as a rejection, but noting in the message body that it is in fact a request for information. The best way to proceed here is responding on the message thread, as the information will be relayed back to the App Review team.


—————————————————


MARKETING
Apple has created a comprehensive Tap to Pay on iPhone Marketing Guide and Toolkit to support you when marketing the availability of Tap to Pay on iPhone to your merchants, partners, and customers.


When marketing Tap to Pay on iPhone in your channels, you should only leverage the Apple-approved marketing assets and templates provided in the Tap to Pay on iPhone Marketing Toolkit.


Please see the Developer Marketing Guidelines for more details on Marketing Requirements.


To access the Tap to Pay on iPhone Marketing Guide and Toolkit, please click here and follow the instructions.
