SCENARIO 1 — The Standard Job Day (Most Common)
Mike owns Mike’s Plumbing. Jake is his worker. Sarah is the client.
Morning — Mike opens the app. Dashboard shows Good morning Mike, 3 jobs scheduled today, weather 28 degrees Cairns, $2,400 outstanding invoices. He taps the Dispatch Board and sees Jake unassigned for the 9am blocked drain at Sarah’s house. He assigns Jake from the board.
Jake’s phone — push notification fires. “New job assigned — Blocked Drain, 14 Maple Street, 9:00am.” Jake opens the app, sees the job on his dashboard with a Start Timer card. He taps navigate and drives to site.
Jake arrives — geofence auto clock-in fires at 100m from address. Timer starts automatically. Jake sees privacy banner “Your location is visible to Mike’s Plumbing while this job is active.”
Mike’s map — Jake appears as a blue marker with “On job: Blocked Drain, Sarah Chen” label.
Jake completes the job — takes before and after photos, adds a note “Cleared root blockage in main drain line”, taps Mark Complete.
Mike — sees job status update to Done in real time. Action Centre fires “1 job done but not invoiced.” He taps Create Invoice, line items pre-fill from the job, sends to Sarah via SMS.
Sarah — receives SMS “Hi Sarah, Mike’s Plumbing has sent you an invoice for $385. Pay securely here: [link]” She taps the link, views the invoice, pays by card online.
Mike — receives push notification “Invoice paid — Sarah Chen $385.” Payment appears in Payment Hub. Jake’s location disappears from the map.
Verify every step works end to end.
