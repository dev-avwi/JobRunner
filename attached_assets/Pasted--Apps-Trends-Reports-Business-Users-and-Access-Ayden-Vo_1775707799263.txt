Thank you for the feedback. We've addressed all three issues:

Guideline 2.1 — NFC:
JobRunner does not read or write NFC tags. The app integrates Stripe Terminal SDK solely for accepting contactless card payments from clients at job sites (Tap to Pay on iPhone). No NFC tag interaction occurs.

Guideline 3.1.1 — Account Registration:
Account registration in JobRunner creates a trade business workspace, not a consumer account. Registration requires a business name, and the onboarding flow collects trade type (Electrical, Plumbing, Carpentry, HVAC, etc.), team size, and ABN. Every screen and feature is specific to managing a trade business — creating job quotes, issuing invoices, dispatching workers, and collecting payments for physical labour and materials. There is no consumer use case for this app.

We have also removed all external payment links, upgrade prompts, and checkout flows from the iOS app. The app displays the business's current plan but contains no mechanism to purchase or upgrade a subscription within the app.

Guideline 3.1.3(c) — Enterprise Services:
JobRunner is a field service management platform for Australian trade businesses — the same category as ServiceM8, Tradify, and Fergus. Subscriptions are purchased by the business entity through our website. The iOS app is a tool for running the business day-to-day, not for purchasing subscriptions. No digital goods or content are sold within the app.
