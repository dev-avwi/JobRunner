Key Findings
JobRunner’s Strength: The web onboarding is exceptionally fast. A user can create an account and generate their first job in
under 3 minutes. The UI is modern (Radix/Tailwind) and highly intuitive.
JobRunner’s Weakness: The absence of published native mobile apps. ServiceM8 dominates because its iOS app is
flawlessly integrated with Apple’s ecosystem (including offline mode). Until JobRunner’s React Native apps are live on the
stores, its mobile UX score remains critically low.
UX Verdict: JobRunner is fundamentally easier to use on the web than its competitors. However, field service software is won and
lost in the field. Publishing the mobile apps and ensuring robust offline capability is the single most important step to unlocking
growth.
