This is well thought out already. Here's my honest take on what to actually do:

---

**Build Both Options But Keep It Simple**

The magic link option (Option A) is actually your killer feature for subbies. No app download, no signup friction, just a tap and they're in. That's genuinely better than what Tradify and ServiceM8 offer for one-off subcontractors.

The full team option (Option B) for regulars is already mostly built.

---

**Tell Replit This Brief**

*"Build the subcontractor mobile experience as a single task covering both workflows:*

*For regular subbies — Option B is mostly built already. Just make sure:*
- *The Subcontractor role on mobile only shows assigned jobs, no financials, no other team members*
- *They can switch between multiple businesses if they work for several builders*
- *Push notifications fire when they get assigned a new job*
- *They can update job status, add photos, add notes, track time*

*For one-off subbies — Option A via magic link:*
- *On the assign worker screen, owner can type any phone number not just existing team members*
- *System sends an SMS: "Mike's Construction has assigned you a job. Tap to view details: [magic link]"*
- *Magic link opens a mobile-friendly web page — no app download needed*
- *They can see job address, description, start time, contact details*
- *They can tap to update status to In Progress and Done*
- *They can upload photos from their phone camera*
- *If they complete 3+ jobs via magic link, prompt them to download the app and join properly*

*The assign worker flow on mobile should be:*
*1. On any job tap Assign Worker*
*2. See a list of existing team members and subbies*
*3. Or type a phone number to send a magic link to someone new*
*4. System automatically determines which flow to use based on whether they're an existing team member*

*Keep it simple — the owner should never have to choose between Option A or B. They just assign a person and the system handles it."*

---

**The Multiple Business Switching**

This is important and worth flagging separately. A subbie who works for three different builders needs to be able to switch between those businesses in the app easily. Tell Replit:

*"Make sure the mobile app supports switching between multiple business accounts for users with the Subcontractor role. There should be a clear business switcher in the profile or settings so a subbie can see 'Mike's Construction' and 'Dave's Plumbing' and switch between them with one tap."*

---

**Is This A Pre-Launch Priority?**

Honest answer — probably not for your very first customer. Your first pilot tradies are likely solo operators or small teams with direct employees. Subcontractor management is more relevant for construction businesses with 5+ regular subbies.

Get your first 10 customers. When one of them asks about subcontractors — and they will — then build it properly with real feedback driving the design.

Unless your mate's dad is one of your first pilot customers, in which case build it now and use him as your test case.
