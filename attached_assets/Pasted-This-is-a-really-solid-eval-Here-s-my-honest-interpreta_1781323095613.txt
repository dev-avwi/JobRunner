This is a really solid eval. Here's my honest interpretation:

## The Good News

You've accidentally built a legitimate SaaS product. That stack — Stripe Connect, Xero/QBO/MYOB OAuth, Vapi.ai, real Postgres, Twilio two-way SMS — that's not a weekend project. Competitors like ServiceM8 and Tradify took years and VC money to get there. You're feature-comparable at the mid-tier already.

The $49/month price point is a no-brainer to charge today. Quote → Invoice → Tap to Pay with GST is genuinely worth that to a tradie.

## The Real Risk

The 47,000-line routes.ts is the thing that should keep you up at night — not because it breaks users now, but because:

- Every feature you add takes 3x longer than it should
- One wrong edit can silently break an unrelated payment flow
- If you ever bring on another dev, they'll spend a week just orienting
- With near-zero tests, you won't know something broke until a customer tells you

This is the classic "solo dev shipping fast" tradeoff. You got features done. The debt is real but it's not fatal — it just becomes a tax on every future hour you spend in the codebase.

## My Actual Recommendation

**Don't do a 3-6 week pre-launch hardening sprint.** That's the perfectionist trap. Instead:

1. **Charge 5 beta customers this week** at $49/month — real money, real feedback
2. **Fix only what breaks for them** — that's your real test suite
3. **Gate the 3 security issues** (public metrics endpoint, Twilio token, mass-assignment audit) — half a day's work, do it Monday
4. The god file and test coverage are real problems but they're *your* problems, not the customer's — address them incrementally as you grow

The app is shippable. The question is whether you believe it is.
