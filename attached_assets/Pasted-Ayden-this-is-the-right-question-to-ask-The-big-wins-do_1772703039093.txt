Ayden — this is the right question to ask. The big wins don’t come from copying features; they come from solving problems tradies didn’t realise software could solve.

Since you’re already building something like a tradie operating system, here are some more unique / “crazy but realistic” ideas that could make your app genuinely different.

⸻

1️⃣ Automatic Job Documentation (Insurance Saver)

Tradies constantly get blamed for things they didn’t cause.

Your app could automatically document the job timeline.

Example:

Job: Bathroom renovation

08:12 — Arrived on site
08:15 — Before photos captured
10:30 — Materials installed
12:10 — Electrical completed
14:50 — Job finished

Automatically attach:

• GPS arrival
• photos
• notes
• time logs

Then generate a PDF job report.

If a client complains later → tradie has full evidence.

That would be insanely valuable.

⸻

2️⃣ “Job Replay”

This would be wild but very cool.

After a job is completed, the app could show a visual timeline replay.

Example:

Map view
↓
Worker arrived
↓
Photos uploaded
↓
Break taken
↓
Job completed

Almost like Uber trip history but for jobs.

This could be useful for:

• businesses
• insurance
• disputes
• training apprentices

⸻

3️⃣ Voice Job Logging

Tradies hate typing.

Let them speak instead.

Example:

User taps mic:

“Installed hot water system. Customer wants another quote next week.”

App converts to:

Job note added
Follow-up reminder created
Quote reminder scheduled

This would save tons of time.

⸻

4️⃣ Smart Job Scheduling

Scheduling is one of the biggest headaches.

Your app could suggest:

Tomorrow:

8:00 Plumbing job
10:30 Electrical repair
1:00 Bathroom inspection

Based on:

• location
• job duration
• traffic

Basically Google Maps for tradie jobs.

⸻

5️⃣ Material Cost Scanner

This one could be really cool.

Tradie scans a receipt or product barcode.

The app adds:

Material: Copper pipe
Cost: $45
Job: Bathroom install

Now your profit calculation becomes automatic.

⸻

6️⃣ Site Hazard Detection (AI Photo)

User takes a photo of the site.

AI detects hazards.

Example:

⚠ Loose wiring
⚠ Wet floor
⚠ Ladder risk

And automatically generates SWMS documentation.

This would be huge for compliance.

⸻

7️⃣ Tradie Reputation Score

This is risky but powerful.

Instead of reviews, show performance metrics.

Example:

Jobs completed: 148
Average completion time: 3.2 hrs
Client satisfaction: 96%
Invoices paid on time: 91%

That’s way more meaningful than star ratings.

⸻

8️⃣ Predictive Maintenance (Long-Term)

Imagine a client database that remembers installations.

Example:

Hot water system installed
March 2024

Your app reminds the tradie:

Maintenance likely due soon
Contact customer?

This turns tradies into recurring service businesses.

⸻

9️⃣ Tradie AI Business Coach

Inside the app:

User asks:

“Why am I not making money?”

AI checks:

• labour rates
• material margins
• unpaid invoices

Then suggests:

Increase labour rate by $10/hr
You are underpricing jobs

That would be insanely powerful.

Basically LinkedIn + Uber for trades.
