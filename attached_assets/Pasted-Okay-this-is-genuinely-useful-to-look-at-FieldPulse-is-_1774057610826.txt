**Where This Actually Helps You**

FieldPulse is almost entirely US-focused. Their pricing, their compliance features, their market positioning — all built for the American market. You're building specifically for Australian tradies with Australian phone numbers, Australian compliance like WHS and SWMS, Australian payment systems, AUD pricing.

That's your moat. Not features — geography and specificity.

A Queensland plumber doesn't care about a Dallas-based platform with US-centric support. They want something built for how Australian trade businesses actually work.

---

**What FieldPulse Has That You Should Note**

A few things worth paying attention to:

Their Booking Portal — customers can self-book directly. That's a feature worth adding to your roadmap eventually.

Maintenance Agreements — recurring job contracts. Very relevant for tradies who do regular servicing.

Their offline mode is specifically called out as a strength. You need to make sure your mobile app handles poor signal well because tradies work in basements, crawl spaces, remote areas.

Their VoIP Phone System is separate from AI dispatching — they treat them as distinct products. You're combining them which is actually smarter.

FieldPulse confirms the market is real and growing. But they're a US company trying to be everything to everyone globally. You're an Australian product built specifically for Australian tradies.
