At least one response

• It breaks down a job into steps

• It outlines suitable risk controls

It documents a safe work plan/procedure for a specific job

Question 7 Part B (1)
Explain how to correctly use a fire blanket during an emergency on a construction site.

×
 Learning
Remove it from its container by pulling the tabs. Hold the blanket up to protect yourself and carefully place it over the fire to smother the flames. Once the blanket covers the fire completely, leave it in place and do not move it until the fire is fully extinguished and the area is safe.

Answer
Remove it from its container by pulling the tabs. Hold the blanket up to protect yourself and carefully place it over the fire to smother the flames. Once the blanket covers the fire completely, leave it in place and do not move it until the fire is fully extinguished and the area is safe.
Question 7 Part B (2)
Describe how to safely use a fire extinguisher on a construction site when fighting a fire.

×
 Learning
The extinguisher should be used by pulling the safety pin, aiming the nozzle at the base of the fire, squeezing the handle, and sweeping side to side until the fire is extinguished.

Answer
The extinguisher should be used by pulling the safety pin, aiming the nozzle at the base of the fire, squeezing the handle, and sweeping side to side until the fire is extinguished.
Question 7 Part B (3)
Describe how to use a fire hose reel on a construction site when fighting a fire.

×
 Learning
To operate it, turn on the main water valve, pull the hose toward the fire, and aim the nozzle at the base of the flames while opening the nozzle valve. Keep the water flowing in a sweeping motion until the fire is out.

Answer
To operate it, turn on the main water valve, pull the hose toward the fire, and aim the nozzle at the base of the flames while opening the nozzle valve. Keep the water flowing in a sweeping motion until the fire is out.
