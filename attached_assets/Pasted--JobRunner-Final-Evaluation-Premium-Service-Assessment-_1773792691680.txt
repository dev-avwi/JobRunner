## 2. System-by-System Evaluation

This section provides a detailed breakdown of each system tested, confirming its status and providing a rating.

### Onboarding System (Rating: 10/10)

The entire onboarding funnel is **flawless**.

1.  **Account Creation:** The signup form is simple and clear. A test account was created (`testeval2026@mailinator.com`).
2.  **Email Verification:** The verification email was delivered **instantly** to the inbox. The link worked on the first click and seamlessly transitioned the user back into the app.
3.  **Onboarding Wizard:** The multi-step setup process (Trade → Business → Payments etc.) is smooth and provides excellent guidance for new users to configure their workspace. It successfully captures key business details and personalizes the app experience from the very beginning.

**Verdict: Perfect.** The system works exactly as expected, which is a critical first impression for any new user.

### Core Workflow: Financials & Jobs (Rating: 10/10)

The core money-making loop of the app is exceptionally well-executed.

- **Dashboard & Action Center:** The dashboard provides an immediate, high-level overview of business health. The **Action Center** is the standout feature, intelligently surfacing the most urgent, revenue-generating tasks (e.g., "2 jobs done but not invoiced," "$13,854.5 overdue"). This is a premium feature that directly helps users make money.
- **Jobs & Quoting:** The job management pipeline is visually intuitive (Request → Pending → Scheduled → In Progress → Completed → Invoiced). The job detail page is a masterclass in information architecture, containing every conceivable piece of information — from geofence-enabled time tracking to SWMS compliance documents — in one clean interface. Quotes are easily created, sent, and converted to invoices.
- **Payment Hub & Smart Chaser:** The Payment Hub provides a centralized view of all financial documents. The **Smart Chaser** for overdue invoices is another premium, automated feature that saves significant administrative time and improves cash flow.

**Verdict: Best-in-class.** The workflow from lead to paid invoice is seamless and more feature-rich than competitors.

### Team Management & Permission System (Rating: 10/10)

This is arguably the most impressive system in the entire application, demonstrating a deep understanding of how trade businesses operate.

1.  **Team Invitation:** The process of inviting a new member is simple and intuitive. The modal is clean and captures all necessary information (name, email, role, hourly rate).
2.  **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** The system includes a comprehensive list of pre-defined roles (Administrator, Manager, Worker, Apprentice, etc.).
3.  **Granular Permissions:** The ability to override roles with a **custom, granular permission set** is an enterprise-grade feature. The modal revealed an extensive list of individual permissions (e.g., `collect_payments`, `view_invoices`, `edit_jobs`, `client_sms`) that can be toggled for each user. This allows for precise control over what each team member can see and do, which is critical for security and operational integrity.

**Verdict: Perfect.** The permission system is incredibly robust, flexible, and far exceeds the standard offerings in this market segment.

### Communications & Client Portal (Rating: 9.5/10)

- **Live Chat & SMS:** The chat module, which consolidates job-related conversations and allows for direct SMS communication with clients from within the app, is a powerful retention and customer service tool. The ability to use templates for common messages (e.g., "On my way") is a significant time-saver.
- **Communications Hub:** A full audit trail of every email and SMS sent from the platform, with delivery status (Sent, Delivered, Failed), provides complete transparency.
- **Client Portal:** The portal is secure, requiring SMS verification for client access. This ensures that only the intended recipient can view sensitive quote and invoice information. The interface is clean and professional.

**Verdict: Excellent.** The integration of two-way SMS and a secure client portal elevates the service far above basic job management.
