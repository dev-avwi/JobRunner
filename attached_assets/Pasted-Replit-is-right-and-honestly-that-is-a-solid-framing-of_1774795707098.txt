The One Thing Replit Did Not Say
The gap between "I built this" and "I make money from this" is distribution — getting it in front of the right people. That is the skill to develop now. Not more building. Go find 10 tradies, show them the app, watch them use it, and listen to what they say. That feedback loop is worth more than any feature you could add.
You have already done the hardest technical work. The next chapter is sales and distribution — and that is a learnable skill, just like coding.
