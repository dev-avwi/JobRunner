JobRunner Document Audit Report:
Quotes, Invoices & Receipts
Overview
This report details the findings from a comprehensive audit of JobRunner’s document
generation system, specifically focusing on Quotes, Invoices, and Receipts. The audit
evaluated the visual layout, data accuracy, PDF generation capabilities, and strict
compliance with Australian Taxation Office (ATO) requirements for valid tax invoices.
1. ATO Compliance Assessment (Critical)
In Australia, the ATO has strict requirements for what constitutes a valid “Tax Invoice”.
If a document does not meet these requirements, the customer cannot claim GST
credits, and legally, they may be required to withhold 47% of the payment (No ABN
Withholding rule).
The Missing ABN (High Priority Fix)
Across all three document types (Quotes, Invoices, Receipts), the Business ABN is
completely missing from the document header.
ATO Requirements Checklist for Sales > $1,000:
Document is intended to be a tax invoice (Clearly labeled “TAX INVOICE”)
Seller’s identity (Business name is present)
Seller’s Australian business number (ABN) (MISSING)
Date the invoice was issued
Brief description of the items sold, including quantity and price
GST amount payable (Correctly calculated at 10% and shown separately)
Buyer’s identity (Client name/address)
The Fix: You must add an ABN field to the Business Settings profile, make it mandatory
for Australian accounts, and render it directly under the Business Name/Address block
on every PDF and web view (e.g., “ABN: 12 345 678 901”).
2. Document-by-Document Breakdown
Quotes
What works well:
Clean, professional layout that builds trust.
Line items are clearly broken down with Qty, Unit Price, and Amount.
GST is calculated correctly (10% of subtotal).
Terms & Conditions and Warranty blocks are included by default, which protects
the tradie legally.
The Signature Block at the bottom (“Quote Acceptance”) is a great touch for
formal approvals.
Issues found:
“Loading…” Bug: In the web view, the “QUOTE FOR” section gets stuck on
“Loading…” instead of displaying the client’s name and address. This appears to
be a React state or data fetching issue that needs immediate fixing.
Missing ABN in the header.
Invoices
What works well:
Correctly titled “TAX INVOICE” (crucial for ATO compliance).
Includes both “Date” and “Due Date”.
Job Site Location is explicitly stated, which is excellent for trade businesses
where the billing address might differ from the site address.
UI features for “Payment Milestones” and “Payment Plans” are visible and wellintegrated.
Issues found:
“Loading…” Bug: Similar to Quotes, the “BILL TO” section is stuck on
“Loading…”.
Missing ABN.
The default email in the demo ( admin@avwebinnovation.com ) should be
dynamic based on the business profile.
Receipts
What works well:
Clearly titled “RECEIPT” with a “Paid” badge.
Shows the exact payment method (e.g., Cash, Stripe).
Includes the Transaction ID for easy reconciliation.
Date & Time stamp of the payment is accurate.
Issues found:
Missing ABN.
The “RECEIVED FROM” section just says “Customer” instead of the actual client’s
name.
3. Public Portal & PDF Generation
Public Portal Routes: The public-facing document routes ( /portal/quote/:token ,
/i/:token , /r/:token ) are successfully implemented and return 200 OK statuses.
This means clients can view their documents online without needing to log in, which is
the correct architecture.
PDF Generation: The PDF generation relies on endpoints (e.g.,
/api/quotes/:id/pdf ). During testing, direct unauthenticated access correctly
returned a 401 Unauthorized , which is good for security. However, the “Save as PDF”
button in the UI needs to ensure it correctly handles the authentication token when
requesting the blob, and displays a loading state (spinner) while the PDF is being
generated, as server-side PDF generation can take 1-3 seconds.
Summary of Action Items
1. Add ABN Field: Update the database schema and Business Settings UI to capture
the ABN.
2. Update Document Templates: Inject the ABN into the header of all Quotes,
Invoices, and Receipts.
3. Fix the “Loading…” Bug: Investigate why the Client Details (Quote For / Bill To)
are failing to render in the document view component.
4. Fix Receipt Client Name: Ensure the receipt template pulls the actual client
name instead of the hardcoded string “Customer”.
5. Verify PDF Download UX: Ensure the “Save as PDF” button has a clear loading
state so users don’t click it multiple times while waiting for the download to
generate.
Overall, the document design is beautiful and functional. Fixing the ABN issue and the
data-loading bug will make them 100% production-ready and legally compliant.
