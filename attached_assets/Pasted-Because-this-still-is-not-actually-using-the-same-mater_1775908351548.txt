## Why it “can’t look like it”

Because most app implementations fake it with:

* blur
* opacity
* rounded rectangle
* shadow

But the real Apple-like result depends on **very fine balancing** of:

* blur amount
* tint colour
* surface opacity
* edge highlight
* internal contrast
* button integration
* typography weight
* spacing

If even 2 or 3 of those are off, it instantly looks custom instead of native.
