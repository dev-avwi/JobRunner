Ensure anyone you talk to sign an NDA before you hand over your code or detailed documentation. Second, use a proper services agreement with very clear IP ownership clauses, stating that all pre-existing IP and any enhancements remain yours.  
Consider trademarking the name JobRunner if you haven't already, and make sure your copyright in the code and assets is documented.  

Share only what's necessary at each stage. You don't need to hand over the full crown jewels just to get a quote. And given how much you've invested in this already, I really think it's worth having a lawyer. even if it's a brief, an Australian commercial lawyer, to give you some of that paperwork to look over.  

  In Cairns, I would start with Miller Harris Lawyers. They have a clear intellectual property and commercial law practice, which makes them a good fit for software IP, NDAs, and contracts. Also, consider O'Connor Law. They handle commercial law matters. And Devenish Law, a boutique commercial practice. If it were my project, I'd book a one-hour consult with Miller Harris first, specifically ask for help with an NDA, an IP ownership clause in your services agreement, and advice on trademarking job runner. That should give you a solid legal foundation before you share anything further.

Baidam — Brisbane CBD

Baidam is a 100% Australian-owned First Nations IT business, headquartered at Level 19, 167 Eagle Street, Brisbane. They're particularly strong in cybersecurity, application security and IT, rather than being primarily an app-development company.

For JobRunner, I'd consider them for a security/technical audit, especially before you start taking paying customers.

Baidam website

Clevvi Technology — Queensland

Clevvi is a Queensland Indigenous-owned digital and AI consultancy. They have software engineers, architects, project managers and business analysts and work across custom digital solutions, cloud, systems integration and digital transformation.

They're potentially useful if JobRunner needs more than just app development — for example, backend architecture, integrations, scalability or business systems.

Projects in Technology — Hamilton, Brisbane

This one is interesting because they're an Indigenous-led technology project management consultancy based at Hamilton. They specialize in IT implementations, business transformation, project assurance and change management.

They may be particularly useful if you need someone to independently assess where JobRunner is up to and manage the process of getting it launch-ready, rather than simply writing code.

Elry — Brisbane

Elry is a smaller Indigenous-owned technology company focused on software/automation and managed technology services. Their model is deliberately small and senior, with the people scoping the work involved in delivering it. They state that their Indigenous ownership became 100% in April 2026 and that they're Supply Nation Certified.

This could be worth investigating if you prefer dealing directly with a small senior team rather than a big agency.
