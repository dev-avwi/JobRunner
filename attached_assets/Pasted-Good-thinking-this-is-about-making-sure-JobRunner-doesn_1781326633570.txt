Every scenario that could go wrong

GPS & Location

	•	Phone loses signal mid-job (underground, rural, thick walls)
	•	GPS drifts and shows tradie in wrong location
	•	Worker forgets to enable location permissions
	•	Battery saver mode kills background GPS

Time Tracking

	•	Timer keeps running after app crashes
	•	Tradie forgets to start/stop timer
	•	Two timers running at once accidentally
	•	Time doesn’t sync if phone goes offline

Jobs & Scheduling

	•	Two workers booked to same job at same time
	•	Job assigned to worker who’s already on another job

Payments & Invoicing

	•	Stripe Tap to Pay fails on site (no signal)
	•	Invoice sent but never received by customer
	•	Duplicate invoice generated
	•	Xero sync fails silently

AI Receptionist

	•	Vapi call drops mid-conversation
	•	AI books a job for a time that’s already taken
	•	Customer leaves voicemail but it never creates a job

SMS & Comms

	•	Twilio SMS fails to deliver
	•	Customer replies to automated SMS but no one sees it
	•	Wrong customer gets someone else’s job details

Photos & Documents

	•	Photo upload fails on slow connection
	•	SWMS submitted without required hazard fields filled
	•	Job completed but no photos attached as proof

Offline

Replit Prompt

Paste this:

This is a read-only audit. Do not change anything. I need you to check how JobRunner handles failure scenarios that would affect tradies on job sites. For each scenario below, tell me: does the app handle it, handle it partially, or not at all?
GPS & Location
	1.	Phone loses GPS signal mid-job — does the app handle location loss gracefully or crash/freeze?
	2.	Worker hasn’t granted location permissions — is there a prompt or error shown?
	3.	Does GPS tracking have any fallback if background location is killed by battery saver?
Time Tracking
4. If the app crashes while a timer is running, does the timer survive or reset to zero?
5. Can two job timers run simultaneously on the same worker account?
6. If a worker goes offline, does the timer continue locally and sync when back online?
Jobs & Scheduling
7. Is there any validation preventing two workers being double-booked to the same time slot?
8. Can a job be created with no address or no assigned worker — and if so, what happens?
9. If a job is cancelled, is the assigned worker notified automatically?
Payments & Invoicing
10. If Stripe Tap to Pay fails (no signal), is there a fallback or retry flow?
11. Is there any duplicate invoice prevention — what stops the same job being invoiced twice?
12. If Xero sync fails, is the error surfaced to the user or does it fail silently?
AI Receptionist & Comms
13. If a Vapi call drops, is there any recovery or callback mechanism?
14. If the AI Receptionist books a job at an already-taken time slot, is there a conflict check?
15. If a Twilio SMS fails to deliver, is the sender notified of the failure?
Photos & Documents
16. If a photo upload fails mid-upload (poor signal), is the user notified and can they retry?
17. Can a SWMS/WHS form be submitted with required hazard fields empty?
18. Can a job be marked complete without any photos attached — and is there a warning?
Offline
19. If a tradie has zero signal, can they still view their assigned jobs and job details?
20. Is there any offline mode or local caching of job data?
