Pay-As-You-Go Managed Server Plan
Pay-As-You-Go Plans provide you Managed Mac accounts and you will prepay for the the time of usage with automatic credit refill. Pay-As-You-Go plans are best for users who use the server infrequently and wish to quickly start development and tasks with pre-installed Mac Applications and Tools. If you need frequent system access, please review and select from our Managed Server Plans.

These plans DO NOT provide you administrator/root access. You may submit support requests for installing additional applications and tools that require administrator access. Upon approval of these requests, our administrators will perform the installation for you. Notice that majority of App Store Apps and Mac Apps do not require administrator access to be installed.

If you wish to obtain server's administrator/root access, the Managed Server Plans are not the right plans for your needs. Instead, please review and select from our Dedicated Server Plans.
