*Apple rejects apps that request permissions without proper justification strings. Check every permission:*

- *All Android permissions declared in app.json — READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION, CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO, NFC, VIBRATE, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED*

*These are the most common rejection reasons:*

- *No placeholder content visible — no lorem ipsum, no test data in production*

- *Demo account credentials provided in review notes so Apple can test the full app*
