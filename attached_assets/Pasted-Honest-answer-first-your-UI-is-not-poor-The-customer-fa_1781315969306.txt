Honest answer first: your UI is not poor. The customer-facing dashboard (Mike's Plumbing) is genuinely solid — clear hierarchy, the "$13,917 owed" hero card is exactly the right thing to lead with, and the Action Centre is a smart feature. This is already better than a lot of shipped trade software (ServiceM8, Tradify territory). A full redesign would be wasted money and risk — Replit rebuilds tend to break things.

So my recommendation is option B: keep the structure, fix specific weaknesses. The admin console is the weaker of the two screens, and there are a handful of polish issues on both.

## What's actually wrong (specifics, not vibes)

**Admin console (dark):**
- Double navigation — there's a left sidebar (Overview, Revenue, Users…) *and* a horizontal tab bar with mostly the same items plus more. Pick one. The tab bar is also overflowing off-screen ("He…" is cut off), which is the single most "unfinished" thing visible.
- The users table has dead columns — Business and Trade are mostly "–". Empty columns make a table feel broken.
- Dates wrap awkwardly ("02 Jun 2026" across three lines). Fixed-width date column, single line.
- Destructive delete (red trash) sits one accidental click away from view/edit with no visual separation.
- Actions column sits between Name and Email — actions conventionally go last.

**Customer dashboard (light):**
- "Revenue $0" and "This Week $0" directly under "$13,917 owed" is contradictory at a glance — if those are zero because of a date filter, label the period ("Revenue (this month)").
- The Action Centre and the Today's Schedule panel both shout about overdue invoices — duplication. Today's Schedule should show today's schedule; when it's empty say "No jobs scheduled today" with a + button, not repeat the invoice warning.
- Six stat cards with mixed icon colours feels a bit carnival; jobs Today "0" probably shouldn't get equal billing with money metrics.
- Sidebar has 14 items flat — grouping (Work / Money / People / Insights) would help, but that's optional.

## Questions worth asking Replit (only the useful ones)

1. Is the design built on a component library (shadcn/ui, Tailwind components?) with a central theme file, or are colours/spacing hardcoded per page? (Tells you whether small changes are safe.)
2. Why does the admin console have both a sidebar and a tab bar — are they wired to the same routes? Can the tab bar be removed without losing pages?
3. Are the dark-mode colours derived from the same design tokens as light mode, or is the admin console a separately styled area? (The two screens look like different products right now.)
4. What's driving "Revenue $0" on the dashboard when $13,917 is owed — what date range is it computing?
