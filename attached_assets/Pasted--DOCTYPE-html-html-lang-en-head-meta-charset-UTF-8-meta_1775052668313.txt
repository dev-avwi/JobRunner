<div class="section">
  <div class="label">01 — The vision</div>
  <h2>What this product actually is</h2>
  <p class="subtitle">The one paragraph that every engineering decision flows from. Replit should read this before touching anything.</p>

  <div class="card locked">
    <div class="decision-title">The product vision — locked</div>
    <div class="decision-body">AppScout is an AI-powered app quality analyser. The user pastes their app URL and records a walkthrough of their own app. The system replays that recording inside a live browser session, streams the video directly to the user's dashboard, and simultaneously runs Claude vision analysis on every screen as it's visited. Handwritten-style annotations draw themselves directly onto the live video at the exact moment an issue is found. A one-line subtitle appears at the bottom of the video telling the user what the AI is looking at right now. When the session ends the user gets four exports — raw video, annotated video, full PDF report, and individual screenshots. The experience is immersive, premium, and unlike anything that currently exists. Nobody has built this combination of live browser streaming, self-drawing annotations, simultaneous AI analysis, and a complete export suite.</div>
    <div class="decision-note">This is the north star. Every engineering decision that conflicts with this vision needs to be flagged and discussed before proceeding.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="card-grid">
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">What it is</div>
      <div class="decision-body">An AI that navigates your app, finds design and UX problems in real time, draws attention to them visually, and hands you a complete report you can act on or share with a client.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">What it is not</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Not a crash tester. Not a performance monitor. Not a session recorder for real users. Not a static screenshot uploader. None of those exist in this product.</div>
    </div>
  </div>

<div class="section">
  <div class="label">02 — Locked decisions</div>
  <h2>Every decision confirmed, nothing left open</h2>
  <p class="subtitle">These were all confirmed. Replit does not need to make any of these decisions. They are already made.</p>

  <!-- Flow recorder -->
  <h3 style="margin-bottom:8px;color:var(--accent)">Flow recorder</h3>

  <div class="card2 locked">
    <div class="decision-title">How recording works — locked</div>
    <div class="decision-body">User pastes their app URL. The app loads inside the AppScout dashboard in an embedded browser. They click Start recording. From that moment every scroll, click, form input, hover, and navigation event is captured. They use their app completely normally. A floating toolbar shows recording duration and a manual screenshot button — pressing it flags that screen as a priority for the AI to analyse carefully. When done they click Stop recording. They see a real-time preview of everything that was captured as they recorded, then a full replay before submitting. If they're unhappy with the recording they can redo it completely. One-click redo, no penalty.</div>
    <div class="decision-note">The recording is stored as a Playwright trace file. On submit it gets queued as a crawl job and replayed exactly.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="card2 locked">
    <div class="decision-title">Pre-recording tips overlay — locked</div>
    <div class="decision-body">Before the recording starts a tips overlay appears for 3 seconds. Tips: Go slowly — pause on each screen for 2-3 seconds. Click everything you want analysed. Use the screenshot button to flag important moments. Navigate through your main user flows. The overlay auto-dismisses and recording begins. Tips are dismissable immediately if the user has seen them before.</div>
  </div>

  <!-- Annotation style -->
  <h3 style="margin-bottom:8px;margin-top:24px;color:var(--accent)">Annotation style — the most important design decision</h3>

  <div class="card locked">
    <div class="decision-title">Self-drawing annotations — locked</div>
    <div class="decision-body">Every annotation draws itself onto the screen like handwriting. No pen visible — just the annotation appearing. The shape draws first (circle, arrow, or underline depending on issue type), then the label text appears letter by letter after the shape finishes. Total animation duration 600-800 milliseconds per annotation. This is implemented as SVG stroke animation — stroke-dashoffset animates from full path length to zero, making the stroke appear to draw itself. The animation speed makes it feel live and intentional, not like a loading state.</div>
    <div class="decision-note">This animation is the signature visual moment of the product. It is also the latency buffer — the 700ms drawing time masks any slight delay between Claude finishing analysis and the annotation appearing. One mechanism doing two jobs.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="card-grid">
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Annotation colours</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Red — critical issues. Amber — warnings. Green — passed checks. Each has its own SVG stroke colour and label background. Consistent with severity system throughout the product.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Annotation shapes</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Circle — draws around a problematic element. Arrow — points to a specific location. Underline — highlights text copy issues. Shape chosen automatically based on issue type returned in Claude JSON.</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Subtitle system -->
  <h3 style="margin-bottom:8px;margin-top:24px;color:var(--accent)">Subtitle system — not a commentator</h3>

  <div class="card2 locked">
    <div class="decision-title">Subtitles not commentary — locked</div>
    <div class="decision-body">No narration. No full commentary track. Just clean one-line subtitles at the bottom of the video like a documentary. Maximum 8 words per subtitle. Appears when the AI starts looking at a new screen or finds something worth surfacing. Fades out after 3 seconds. Never competes with what the user is watching. Font is clean and slightly editorial — DM Sans medium weight with generous letter spacing. Subtle dark pill background behind the text for readability over any app colour.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="card2 locked">
    <div class="decision-title">Status indicator — top right of video</div>
    <div class="decision-body">Three states only: Navigating (when Playwright is moving between screens), Analysing (when Claude is processing a screenshot), Issue found (brief flash with colour pulse when annotation is about to appear). That is all. No other text in the video area.</div>
  </div>

  <!-- Video sync -->
  <h3 style="margin-bottom:8px;margin-top:24px;color:var(--accent)">Video sync and pause behaviour</h3>

  <div class="card locked">
    <div class="decision-title">The pause design — locked</div>
    <div class="decision-body">The 2-3 second hold on each new screen is not a loading pause. It is a designed moment. During the hold: the subtitle shows what the AI is looking at, the status shows Analysing, and the annotation begins drawing itself if the analysis is ready. The hold feels like the AI taking a breath to look properly. If it is designed correctly the user never perceives it as waiting — they perceive it as the AI working. The pause must always have visual life. It is never an empty frozen screen.</div>
    <div class="decision-note">Maximum hold is 3 seconds regardless of whether analysis has returned. If Claude is slow the annotation arrives late and attaches to the timeline at the correct timestamp. The video always resumes at 3 seconds. No exceptions.</div>
  </div>

  <!-- Credit system -->
  <h3 style="margin-bottom:8px;margin-top:24px;color:var(--accent)">Credit and pricing system</h3>

  <div class="card locked">
    <div class="decision-title">Token-based subscription — locked direction</div>
    <div class="decision-body">Subscription plans include a monthly token allowance. Tokens are spent per scan based on scan type and screen count. Scan types and approximate costs: Full app scan — 100 tokens (up to 30 screens), Scenario scan — 50 tokens (specific user flow), Quick audit — 20 tokens (single screen or small section). Exact token costs to be calibrated once the analysis pipeline is built and real API costs are known — do not hardcode these until then. Top-up packs available for purchase when monthly allowance is exhausted. This follows the Originality.ai model — familiar and understood by developers.</div>
    <div class="decision-note">Token costs must be calibrated against real Claude API costs with a margin. Do not finalise token pricing until the pipeline is built and real costs measured.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="card-grid-3">
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Indie — $29/mo</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Monthly token allowance for solo developers. Full scan, scenario, and quick audit types included. Shareable report link included.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Team — $99/mo</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Larger token allowance. Up to 5 users. All scan types. Priority analysis queue. PDF export included.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Agency — $249/mo</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Largest token allowance. Unlimited users. White label report option. Client shareable links with agency branding. Priority support.</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Exports -->
  <h3 style="margin-bottom:8px;margin-top:24px;color:var(--accent)">Export suite</h3>

  <div class="card-grid">
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Raw video — MP4</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Clean recording of the browser session with no overlays. Compressed to under 50MB via H.264 CRF 28. Web quality default, full quality option with file size shown before downloading.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Annotated video — MP4</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Full session with annotation overlays and subtitle text baked in. FFmpeg merges the canvas layer with the raw video. This is the primary shareable asset — plays anywhere without special software.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Full report — PDF + web</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Every screen thumbnailed. Issues listed by severity with exact location, problem, and fix. Overall score. Breakdown by category and pass type. This is what agencies send to clients.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="card2 locked">
      <div class="decision-title">Screenshots — ZIP</div>
      <div class="decision-body">Every captured screen as a clean PNG with no overlays. Useful for dropping into Figma, design tools, or Jira tickets. Named by screen path for easy organisation.</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="card2 locked">
    <div class="decision-title">Shareable link</div>
    <div class="decision-body">Generates a unique public URL. Anyone can view the annotated video and full report without an account. Link served from a hosted public report page. Unique token stored in PostgreSQL against the run. Free tier links expire after 30 days. Paid tier links permanent. The shareable link is the primary organic growth mechanic — users share their audit results and others discover the product.</div>
  </div>

  <h3 style="color:var(--red);margin-bottom:10px">Blockers — solve before building anything else</h3>

  <div class="prob critical-card">
    <div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-red" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Blocker 1</span> SPA navigation not triggering events</div>
    <div class="prob-body">React/Vue/Angular apps change content without full page navigation. waitForNavigation never fires. Crawler sits on first screen forever.</div>
    <div class="prob-sol">Watch two signals: URL hash/path changes via History API AND significant DOM mutations via MutationObserver. Either triggers screen discovery. Debounce DOM mutations 500ms.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="prob critical-card">
    <div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-red" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Blocker 2</span> Screenshot capturing half-loaded screens</div>
    <div class="prob-body">Screenshot fires immediately on navigation. Heavy JS apps take 2-4 seconds to render. Claude analyses a skeleton screen and gives useless feedback.</div>
    <div class="prob-sol">Two-condition wait: networkidle (no requests for 500ms) AND DOM stability (no mutations for 300ms). Both required. Cap at 8 seconds maximum.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="prob critical-card">
    <div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-red" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Blocker 3</span> Bot protection blocking the crawler</div>
    <div class="prob-body">Cloudflare, Imperva, and other CDN protection blocks the crawler within seconds. Product appears broken with no helpful error.</div>
    <div class="prob-sol">Playwright stealth mode plugin. Detect block pages via known CAPTCHA selectors or 403/429 responses. Never silent fail — clear message with IP whitelist instructions or manual upload fallback.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="prob critical-card">
    <div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-red" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Blocker 4</span> Auth flows and session expiry mid-crawl</div>
    <div class="prob-body">Long scans on apps with short session timeouts. User gets logged out mid-crawl. All subsequent screens are the login page.</div>
    <div class="prob-sol">Monitor each screenshot for login page indicators. If detected mid-crawl, auto re-authenticate using stored credentials. If re-auth fails, pause and notify via WebSocket with option to update credentials.</div>
  </div>

  <div class="prob critical-card">
    <div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-red" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Blocker 5</span> Claude rate limits during parallel analysis</div>
    <div class="prob-body">120 API calls in minutes from parallel analysis across multiple users hits rate limits. Jobs fail silently or return errors stored as results.</div>
    <div class="prob-sol">Exponential backoff with jitter. Analysis queue concurrency capped below rate limit threshold. Failed jobs retry up to 3 times. Rate limit errors never stored as results — always trigger retry.</div>
  </div>

  <h3 style="color:var(--amber);margin-bottom:10px;margin-top:24px">Handle during build — standard engineering practice</h3>

  <div class="prob warn-card"><div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-amber" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Warning 6</span> Cookie consent banners</div><div class="prob-body">Banner obscures content. All analysis is about the cookie UI not the product.</div><div class="prob-sol">Detect common consent selectors (OneTrust, Cookiebot). Auto-click accept.   <div class="prob warn-card"><div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-amber" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Warning 7</span> Infinite scroll and pagination loops</div><div class="prob-body">Crawler scrolls or paginates forever. Never finishes.</div><div class="prob-sol">pHash catches repeated content at 90% similarity. Cap screenshots per domain path at 5.   <div class="prob warn-card"><div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-amber" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Warning 8</span> External links taking crawler off-domain</div><div class="prob-body">Crawler follows links to documentation, social media, partner sites.</div><div class="prob-sol">Intercept all navigation.   <div class="prob warn-card"><div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-amber" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Warning 9</span> File download dialogs blocking navigation</div><div class="prob-body">Download dialog triggered. Playwright hangs. Crawl freezes.</div><div class="prob-sol">Intercept all download events. Cancel automatically. Log as "download trigger found" in report.</div></div>

  <div class="prob info-card"><div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-blue" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Info 14</span> Annotation coordinates misaligned on different screen sizes</div><div class="prob-body">Accessibility tree returns coordinates at original browser viewport size.   <div class="prob info-card"><div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-blue" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Info 19</span> App requires localhost or VPN</div><div class="prob-body">Cloud crawler can't reach staging or localhost environments.</div><div class="prob-sol">V1: document clearly in onboarding.   <div class="prob info-card"><div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-blue" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Info 20</span> Playwright memory leak on long runs</div><div class="prob-body">Browser context accumulates memory on 50+ screen apps. Monitor memory per worker — reset context at 500MB threshold.</div></div>

  <div class="prob info-card"><div class="prob-title"><span class="tag t-blue" style="margin:0;font-size:10px">Info 21</span> S3 storage costs unbounded</div><div class="prob-body">Hundreds of PNGs and two MP4s per scan.
