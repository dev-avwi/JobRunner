Carpet in Cairns
Carpet flooring provides warmth, comfort and style, making it a popular choice for homes and businesses alike. With a variety of textures, colours and patterns, it complements any décor.



Carpet also reduces noise and improves insulation, creating a cosy environment. Comfortable and soft underfoot, it’s ideal for living spaces and bedrooms.

Vinyl in Cairns
Vinyl flooring is a versatile and cost-effective option, perfect for both residential and commercial spaces. It is durable, water-resistant and easy to maintain, making it ideal for high-traffic areas.



Available in a wide range of colours, patterns and textures, vinyl can mimic the look of wood, stone, or tile while offering superior comfort and affordability.

Cork in Cairns
Cork is a sustainable and versatile flooring material made from the bark of cork oak trees. It offers natural insulation, reducing noise and maintaining comfortable temperatures.



Cork is also highly durable, resistant to moisture and provides a soft, cushioned surface underfoot. Its eco-friendly properties and stylish appearance make it ideal for both residential and commercial spaces.

Comcork in Cairns
Comcork is a durable, eco-friendly flooring solution made from recycled cork and rubber. It offers excellent slip resistance, even in wet conditions, making it ideal for high-traffic areas in commercial applications.



Known for its resilience, comfort and low maintenance, Comcork is a versatile choice that combines sustainability with outstanding performance and safety.

Marine in Cairns
Marine flooring is specially designed to withstand the unique challenges of marine environments, including exposure to water, salt and UV rays.



﻿Durable, slip-resistant and easy to clean, marine flooring provides safety and comfort for boats, yachts, and other watercraft. Available in various materials and styles, it combines functionality with aesthetic appeal.
