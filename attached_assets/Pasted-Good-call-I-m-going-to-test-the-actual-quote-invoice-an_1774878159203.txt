The One Critical Issue: Missing ABN
Every single document is missing the business ABN. This is not a cosmetic issue — it is a legal one.
Under ATO rules, a "Tax Invoice" without an ABN is not a valid tax invoice. This means:
The tradie's customer cannot claim GST credits from their accountant.
If the customer is a business, they are legally required to withhold 47% of the payment (the No ABN Withholding rule).
The tradie could receive complaints from their commercial clients saying "your invoice is invalid."
The fix is straightforward: Add an ABN field to Business Settings, make it mandatory, and render it under the business name on every document. One line: ABN: 12 345 678 901.
The Two Bugs to Fix
1. "Loading..." stuck on Quote and Invoice views. The "Quote For" and "Bill To" sections are rendering "Loading..." instead of the client's name and address. This is a React data-fetching timing issue — the component is rendering before the client data has resolved. It needs a proper loading state or the data needs to be included in the initial document fetch.
2. Receipt says "Customer" instead of the client's name. The receipt template has a hardcoded string "Customer" in the "Received From" field. It needs to pull the actual client name from the payment record.
