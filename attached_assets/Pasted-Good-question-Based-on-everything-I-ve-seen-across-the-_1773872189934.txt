Good question. Based on everything I've seen across the whole app, here's what I'd go through before touching the AI receptionist:

---

**Things Worth Going Through Properly**

**1. The Full Job-to-Payment Flow**

This is the most important one. Do the entire flow yourself right now as if you're a tradie:

Create a job → schedule it → mark in progress → complete it → convert to invoice → send invoice → mark as paid

If anything feels clunky or confusing at any step, fix it. This is the flow tradies will use every single day and it needs to be flawless.

**2. Quote Flow End to End**

Create a quote → add line items → send it to a real email address → open the client portal link → approve it → see it convert to a job

Actually send it to your own email and go through it as a client would. Does the email look professional? Does the portal load fast? Can you approve it easily on mobile?

**3. Invoice and Payment Flow**

Create an invoice → send it → pay it using a test card in Stripe

Check that the payment confirmation looks right, the invoice updates to paid, and the dashboard reflects it.

**4. Team Member Experience**

Invite a fake worker using a second email address. Log in as that worker. What do they see? Can they only see their assigned jobs? Does the limited permissions view make sense or is it confusing?

**5. Mobile App on a Real Device**

Not the simulator — a real phone. Go through the same job flow on mobile. Check:
- Does it load fast?
- Are buttons easy to tap?
- Does the keyboard cover input fields?
- Does it work on both iOS and Android?

**6. Client Portal on Mobile**

Send yourself a quote link and open it on your phone. Most tradies' customers will open this on a phone not a desktop. Does it look right?

**7. Notifications**

Create a job, complete it, send an invoice. Do the push notifications actually fire on the mobile app? Do the SMS notifications send correctly from the shared Twilio number?

**8. Integrations Personally Verified**

As we discussed — connect a real Xero demo account yourself and run through the sync. Same with Google Calendar. Don't trust Replit's automated tests on this.

**9. Settings and Onboarding**

Sign up with a completely fresh email address you've never used. Go through the onboarding wizard as a brand new user. Does it feel smooth? Does the business setup make sense? How long does it take to get to your first job?

Time it. If it takes more than 5 minutes to go from signup to first job created, it needs simplifying.

**10. Demo Account Cleanup**

The things we flagged earlier:
- Remove the 12 failed SMS messages from the old Replit dev URL
- Remove duplicate templates in Templates Hub
- Fix the doubled Edit SWMS header
- Make sure all demo jobs and data look realistic and complete, not half-finished

---

**Things That Are Lower Priority Right Now**

These matter eventually but don't block the AI receptionist build:

- App Store submission
- Advanced reporting
- Zapier integration
- MYOB integration
- Multi-branch support

---

**The Honest Filter**

For each thing you find, ask yourself one question:

*"Would this stop a tradie from using the app or paying for it?"*

If yes — fix it now.
If no — put it on a list and do it after launch.

The duplicate SWMS header — yes, fix it, looks broken.
The WHS tab overflow — borderline, worth fixing.
Quote version history — genuinely useful but not blocking.
The empty form preview — annoying but not blocking.

---

**After You've Done All of That**

Once you've personally gone through every flow above and fixed what you found — that's when you're ready to hand Replit the Retell brief and start the AI receptionist build.

Realistically that's probably 3-5 days of focused testing and fixing. Do it properly and you'll go into the AI build with confidence that the foundation is solid.
