Hello App Review,

Thank you for the follow-up. The screenshot you provided was extremely helpful — it revealed the root cause.

Root cause identified and fixed:
Our backend had an early-access mechanism that automatically granted lifetime free access to the first 15 signups (a launch promotion). When your reviewer account signed up fresh, our server unintentionally classified it as one of these early-access accounts and granted Pro Lifetime entitlement before any purchase was attempted. This is why your screenshot shows "Founding Member" and "Pro Plan — Lifetime Access" at the top — the account had been auto-entitled. Subsequent IAP purchase attempts then failed because StoreKit could not reconcile the existing entitlement.

Fix shipped (server-side, already live in production):
We have disabled the auto-grant mechanism. New signups — including App Review sandbox tester accounts — no longer receive any free entitlements automatically and must purchase a subscription to access paid features. We have also reset any previously affected reviewer accounts to a clean Free state.

To verify, please:

Sign in to App Store with a fresh sandbox tester account (Settings → App Store → Sandbox Account)
Sign up for a new JobRunner account in the app
The "Founding Member" badge should NOT appear, and the upgrade flow should function normally
Tap "Upgrade to Pro" (or Team or Business) — the StoreKit purchase sheet will appear and the subscription will activate
No new build is required for this fix as it is a server-side change. The current build (1.1.1, build 68) will work correctly with the patched server.
