Here is the full audit report for JobRunner. Both the Markdown and PDF versions are attached below.
The Honest Verdict: 5.8 / 10
JobRunner is a genuinely exceptional product for a beta — but the engineering foundation needs to catch up with the product vision before it can safely scale to 1,000 businesses.
The Big Wins
Feature breadth is remarkable. Native SWMS/WHS compliance, AI Receptionist (Vapi.ai), AI quote generation, geofence clock-in/out, dispatch board, Stripe Connect, and a client/subcontractor portal — all in one app. No competitor offers this combination at this price point.
Pricing model is excellent. The freemium entry with Pro at $39/mo and Team at $49/mo + $29/seat is perfectly calibrated for the AU SMB tradie market.
Modern, clean UI. Radix UI + shadcn/ui + Tailwind is a solid, accessible component foundation.
