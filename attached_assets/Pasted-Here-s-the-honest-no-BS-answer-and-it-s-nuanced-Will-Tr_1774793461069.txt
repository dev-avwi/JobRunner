Will Tradies Switch? The Honest Answer
Yes — but only if you fix 5 specific things first. The product vision is genuinely better than Tradify and ServiceM8. The AI Receptionist, native SWMS/WHS, and AI quote generation are real differentiators that tradies will pay for. But right now, the app has critical gaps that will cause churned trials before they convert.
The 3 Things That Will Kill Adoption Right Now
1. The Mobile Apps Don't Exist
Both the App Store and Google Play links in your code return 404 Not Found. Tradies will search "JobRunner" on the App Store, find nothing, and immediately assume the product isn't real. This is your single biggest adoption blocker.
2. Webhook Security is Broken
When I sent fake Stripe and Xero webhook payloads with invalid signatures, the server returned 200 OK. This means a malicious actor can send a fake "payment succeeded" event and mark invoices as paid without any real money changing hands. This is a financial integrity vulnerability that must be fixed before you take any real payments.
3. No One-Click Migration from Tradify/ServiceM8
You have CSV import endpoints, but no "Import from ServiceM8" button. Asking a busy tradie to manually export, reformat, and upload their data is a deal-breaker. Your competitors that win conversions do the migration for the customer.
