JobRunner (jobrunner.com.au) is a specialized project management and ERP software designed for commercial flooring businesses, now part of Cyncly. It streamlines the entire workflow, including lead tracking, proposals, change orders, and financial,, as well as job costing. 

jobrunner.com.au
 +2
Key Features and Capabilities:
Commercial Flooring Focus: Tailored for the specific needs of commercial flooring contractors.
Project Management: Manages jobs from initial bids to final billing, including tracking changes and progress.
Integrated Finance: Handles job costing and progress billing (installed quantities, fixed amounts, or percentage-based).
Mobile Access: Features an "Installer Mobile" app for real-time scheduling, document access (floor plans), and inventory checks.
Operational Control: Connects team communication with barcode-driven inventory and scheduling calendars.
