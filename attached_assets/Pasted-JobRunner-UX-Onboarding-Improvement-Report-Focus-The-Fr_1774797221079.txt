JobRunner: UX & Onboarding
Improvement Report
Focus: The “Friends & Family” First-Run Experience
Prepared by: Manus AI Date: March 29, 2026
This report provides a prioritised, actionable breakdown of friction points in
JobRunner’s current onboarding flow. The goal is to optimize the experience so that
when you send a link to a non-technical family member or friend (e.g., an uncle
running a landscaping business), they can immediately understand the value and get
to their first “aha” moment without needing you to explain it.
1. The Landing Page Friction
Current State
When a user lands on jobrunner.com.au , they see a clean, professional site. However,
to see the product in action, they must click “Get Started Free”, fill out a registration
form, verify their email, and complete a multi-step setup wizard. For a busy tradie (or a
skeptical family member), this is too much work just to see if the app is any good.
The Solution: One-Click Demo Account
The most impactful change you can make to your conversion rate is adding a “Try the
Demo” button right next to “Get Started Free” [1].
How it works: Clicking the button instantly logs the user into a pre-populated,
read-only (or ephemeral) demo account with realistic data (jobs, quotes,
invoices, team members).
Why it works: It bypasses the entire email verification and setup friction. They
can click around, see the drag-and-drop calendar, view a generated PDF quote,
and understand the product in 30 seconds.
Implementation: Create a generic user account (e.g., demo@jobrunner.com.au )
with seeded data. When a user clicks “Try the Demo”, generate a temporary
session token for that account and redirect them to the dashboard. Add a
persistent banner at the top: “You are viewing a shared demo account. [Create
your own free account to save your data.]”
Messaging & Copy Tweaks
The current messaging uses the word “Beta” prominently (“Beta: First 10 users get
lifetime free access”). To non-technical users, “Beta” sounds like “unfinished” or
“buggy.”
Current: “Beta: First 10 users get lifetime free access”
Recommended: “Early Access: First 10 users get lifetime free access” or
“Founding Member: Lifetime free access”
2. The Email Verification Trap
Current State
After submitting the registration form, users are redirected to a /verify-emailpending page. They must leave the app, open their email client, find the email, click
the link, and then return to the app.
The Friction
Many users drop off here. They might be on their phone, get distracted by another
email, or the verification email might go to spam.
The Solution: Deferred Verification
Do not block the user from entering the app.
1. Allow them to complete the onboarding wizard and enter the dashboard
immediately after registration.
2. Place a prominent banner at the top of the dashboard: “Please verify your email
address (uncle.bob@example.com) to send quotes and invoices. [Resend Email]”
3. This allows them to experience the “Life of a Job” tour and see the value before
you ask them to do the work of verifying their email.
3. The Onboarding Wizard & “Life of a Job”
Current State
The onboarding wizard is actually very strong. The “Life of a Job” walkthrough is an
excellent concept, guiding them through 7 steps (A New Lead Calls -> Schedule -> OnSite -> Quote -> Accept -> Work -> Get Paid).
However, after the tour finishes, they are dropped into a dashboard with demo data
and a setup checklist. The transition from “watching a tour” to “doing it yourself” is a
steep cliff.
The Solution: Guided “First Real Job” Creation
Instead of just leaving them with a setup checklist (which feels like homework), guide
them to create their first real job.
1. The Empty State: When they clear the demo data, the dashboard shouldn’t just
be empty. It should have a massive, unmissable CTA: “Create Your First Job”.
2. Pre-filled Templates based on Trade: During onboarding, you ask for their
Trade Type. Use this! If they selected “Plumber”, the “Create Job” modal should
suggest:
Client: (Add a new client)
Job Title: e.g., “Leaking Tap Repair” or “Hot Water System Install”
Description: “Customer reported water pooling under the sink.”
3. The “Aha” Moment: Guide them to click “Generate Quote” for that job. When
they see a beautifully formatted PDF quote with their business name on it, they
will be hooked.
Checklist Copy Refinement
The current setup checklist items are good, but can be made more action-oriented and
benefit-driven:
Current: “Add your business details” -> Better: “Add your business details (so you
can get paid)”
Current: “Add your logo” -> Better: “Upload your logo to make your quotes look
professional”
Current: “Set up online payments” -> Better: “Connect Stripe to let clients pay by
credit card”
4. The Mobile App Blocker
Current State
The landing page prominently features “Download on the App Store” and “Get it on
Google Play” buttons. As discovered in the technical audit, these links currently return
404 errors.
The Friction
If you text your uncle the link to jobrunner.com.au , the first thing he will do on his
phone is click “Download on the App Store.” When it fails, he will assume the app is
broken or fake, and he won’t bother trying the web version.
The Solution: Immediate Remediation
1. Short Term (Today): Remove the App Store/Play Store buttons from the landing
page. Replace them with a single button: “Open Web App (Works on Mobile)”.
Ensure the web app is fully responsive and behaves like a PWA (Progressive Web
App). Add a prompt in the web app showing them how to “Add to Home Screen”.
2. Long Term (Next 2 Weeks): Getting the Expo/React Native apps approved and
live on the stores is the absolute highest priority for user trust.
Summary of Immediate Actions for Family/Friends
Launch
If you want to send this to family this weekend, do these three things first:
1. Hide the App Store buttons until they are live.
2. Change “Beta” to “Early Access” so it sounds like a privilege, not a test.
3. Create a one-click Demo link (e.g., jobrunner.com.au/demo ) that bypasses
signup entirely, so you can just text them: “Hey, check out this app I built. Click
this link to play around with a demo account, no signup needed.”
