This is actually your biggest legal risk. If the AI says something like "we'll be there at 9am tomorrow" and the tradie doesn't show up, customer could claim a promise was made.

Solutions:

Every AI conversation ends with: "Someone from the team will confirm the details with you shortly"
Never let the AI confirm specific times or prices
Store every call recording and transcript permanently
Add to your Terms of Service: "AI receptionist is for enquiry collection only, not binding agreements"
Scenario 4 — Customer data privacy — this is the serious one

Under Australian Privacy Act you need to be careful storing customer phone numbers and call recordings.

Solutions that protect you:

Add to your Terms of Service and Privacy Policy that businesses using JobRunner are responsible for their own customer data
Show customers a disclosure when the AI answers: "This call may be recorded for quality purposes"
Give businesses a data deletion tool so they can remove client records if requested
Store data in Australian servers if possible — AWS Sydney region
Scenario 5 — Business cancels but customer data still exists

If a business cancels JobRunner, what happens to their customers' data?

Solution:

On cancellation, give the business 30 days to export their data
After 60 days, permanently delete all client records for that account
Document this clearly in your Terms of Service

This puts the legal responsibility on them for their own customers.
