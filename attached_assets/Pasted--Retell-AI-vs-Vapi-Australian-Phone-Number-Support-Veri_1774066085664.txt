# Retell AI vs. Vapi: Australian Phone Number Support Verification

**Date:** March 21, 2026
**Author:** Manus AI

## 1. Executive Summary

This report verifies the capabilities of Retell AI and Vapi to support Australian phone numbers for the purpose of building an AI receptionist for JobRunner. The investigation confirms that **both platforms can be used with Australian Twilio numbers**, but they have different integration methods, advantages, and potential blockers.

**The final recommendation is to use Vapi.** While Retell AI has a latency advantage, Vapi's simpler and more direct Twilio integration process presents a lower risk of setup friction and delays, making it the more practical choice for immediate development.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Native AU Numbers** | No | No | Tie |
| **AU Twilio Integration** | Yes (via SIP Trunk) | Yes (Direct Import) | **Vapi** |

## 2. Retell AI: Australian Number Verification

Retell AI does not offer native Australian phone numbers for purchase. However, it officially supports using Australian numbers from third-party providers like Twilio through a **SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) trunk**. [1]

### Integration Method

The process involves:
1.  Purchasing an Australian number from Twilio.
2.  Creating an "Elastic SIP Trunk" within Twilio.
3.  Configuring the trunk's "Termination" (for outbound calls) and "Origination" (for inbound calls) settings to point to Retell's servers.
4.  Importing the number into the Retell AI dashboard. [2]

This method is technically sound but is more complex than a direct integration.

### Identified Blockers

A critical blocker was identified in a Retell community forum post from March 2026. A user reported that all outbound calls to Australian numbers were failing with a `SIP 403` error, indicating a permission issue. [3]

> **Error from Detail Logs:**
> `Error dialing to user, SIP status code: 403 SIP error category: telephony_provider_permission_denied sip status: 403: Dialed number +61... is not included in whitelisted countries USA,CAN D13`

This error occurred because of an **account-level country whitelist** that defaults to only the US and Canada. The user had to contact Retell support to manually add Australia to their account's whitelist. This represents a significant potential friction point and delay in the setup process, as it relies on manual intervention from the Retell team.

---

## 3. Vapi: Australian Number Verification

Vapi, like Retell, does not offer native Australian numbers. Their documentation explicitly states that free numbers are for US use only and that international numbers must be imported from Twilio. [4]

### Integration Method

Vapi offers a much simpler, more direct integration method:
1.  Purchase an Australian number from Twilio.
2.  Use the "Import" function in the Vapi dashboard.
3.  Enter the Twilio phone number, Account SID, and Auth Token. [5]

Vapi then uses the Twilio credentials to automatically configure the number for use on their platform. This is significantly less complex than the manual SIP trunking process required by Retell.

### Identified Blockers

While the import process is simple, a potential blocker for **inbound calls** was identified in a Skool community post. A user successfully imported an Australian Twilio number and made outbound calls, but inbound calls failed. [6]

The solution was to manually configure the number's webhook settings within the Twilio console. When a call comes in, the webhook must be set to `https://api.vapi.ai/twilio/inbound_call`.

This is a one-time technical configuration step. Unlike the Retell blocker, it does not require waiting for a support team and can be resolved immediately by the developer during setup.

## 4. Head-to-Head Comparison & Recommendation

| Factor | Retell AI | Vapi | Analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Setup Simplicity** | Low | High | Vapi's direct import is far simpler than Retell's manual SIP trunking. |
| **Setup Blockers** | **High Risk.** Requires manual support intervention and waiting for an account-level change. | **Low Risk.** A one-time, self-service configuration fix is required for inbound calls. |
| **Latency** | **Excellent (~800ms).** Optimized for low-latency, which is critical for a natural-sounding receptionist. | **Good (~1000-1500ms).** More flexible but less optimized. May be noticeable in conversation. |
| **Community & Docs** | Good. Official docs are clear, but community posts reveal critical blockers. | Excellent. Larger community and more tutorials available, with clear solutions to known issues. |

### Final Recommendation

For the JobRunner AI Receptionist, **Vapi is the recommended platform to start with.**

The primary reason is the **lower risk of setup friction.** The Retell requirement for manual support intervention to enable outbound Australian calls is a significant, unpredictable blocker. A developer could be stuck for days waiting for a support ticket to be resolved.

Vapi's inbound call configuration is a known, solvable technical step that a developer can handle in minutes. While Retell's lower latency is attractive, the immediate and predictable path to a working Australian number with Vapi makes it the more pragmatic choice to begin development without delay.
