Thank you for the feedback. Background location in JobRunner is used for several features that directly benefit the user (the field worker carrying the device), beyond employee tracking:

Automatic job site check-in/out — When a worker arrives at a job site, the app automatically starts their time tracking clock, and stops it when they leave. This saves the worker from manually clocking in and out throughout the day. (Dashboard → tap a job → "Start" to begin, location is used to detect arrival at the job address)

Turn-by-turn navigation to next job — Workers can tap "Navigate" on any assigned job to get directions to the job site. Background location keeps navigation active while the worker is driving. (Dashboard → tap a job → "Navigate")

Live job sharing with clients — Workers can share a live tracking link with clients so they know when the tradie is on their way, reducing unnecessary calls. This directly benefits the worker's customer communication. (Job detail → Share Job Link)

Background location is only active when the worker has an active job in progress and can be disabled at any time by the worker in their privacy settings. Location sharing requires explicit opt-in and is never enabled by default.
