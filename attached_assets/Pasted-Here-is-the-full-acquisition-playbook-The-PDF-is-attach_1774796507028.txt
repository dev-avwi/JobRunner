The Brutal Truth About Getting Your First 20 Customers
Forget ads, SEO, and social media campaigns for now. None of that works until you have at least 50 happy customers generating word-of-mouth. Your first 20 come from direct, personal, hand-to-hand outreach — and that is actually a massive advantage for you, because you can move faster than any marketing agency.
The 4-Week Sprint
Week 1 — Fix the Blockers First
The mobile apps need to be live on the App Store and Google Play before you pitch anyone. A tradie who cannot search "JobRunner" and find it immediately will not trust the product. This is your single most important task before any outreach.
Week 2 — Facebook Groups (The Right Way)
Do not post a link to your app. Post a pain-point question or offer the AI Receptionist as a free trial. The groups "Tradies in Australia" and "Small Business Support Group Australia" are your best starting points. When people comment, move to DMs immediately and book a 15-minute call.
Week 3 — Direct SMS Outreach
Find 1–5 star trade businesses on Google Maps in your area. Text them directly (not email — tradies don't check cold emails). Keep it under 3 sentences. Offer lifetime free access in exchange for feedback.
Week 4 — White-Glove Onboarding
This is the one most founders skip and it is the most important. When a tradie says yes, you do the setup for them. Import their client list, set up their logo and quote templates, and hand them an account that is 100% ready to use. This removes the biggest adoption barrier entirely.
Pricing for the First 20
Customers 1–5: Free for life. In exchange: a 15-minute feedback call every two weeks and a video testimonial when they love it. These testimonials are worth $10,000 each in future marketing value.
Customers 6–20: $19/month locked in for life (instead of $39). They get a deal, you get paying customers and proof of willingness to pay.
The AI Receptionist Hook: Offer it at $30/month (half price) to any early adopter. Once a tradie has an AI answering their calls and booking jobs while they are on the tools, they will never cancel. It is the stickiest feature you have.
The One Metric That Matters Right Now
Not website visitors. Not signups. Not even MRR.
Active Workspaces — how many accounts actually created a job or sent an invoice this week. That is the only number that tells you whether people are genuinely using the product or just signing up and forgetting about it.
