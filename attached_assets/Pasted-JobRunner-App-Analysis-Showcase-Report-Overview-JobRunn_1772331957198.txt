JobRunner App Analysis & Showcase Report

Overview

JobRunner is a sophisticated, full-stack business management platform designed specifically for the Australian trade industry. It effectively bridges the gap between on-site fieldwork and back-office administration, providing a seamless workflow from initial client request to final payment.




Core Features & Technical Highlights

1. Comprehensive Dashboard & Action Centre

•
Real-time Insights: Provides an immediate overview of business health, including revenue, active jobs, and quotes out.

•
Action Centre: Intelligently identifies "revenue at risk" and pending tasks, ensuring no job or invoice is forgotten.

•
Today's Schedule: A clear, chronological view of the day's work with direct links to maps and job details.

2. Advanced Job Management

•
Status Tracking: A visual pipeline from "Request" and "Scheduled" to "In Progress," "Completed," and "Invoiced."

•
On-Site Tools: Includes "On My Way" notifications, job-specific chat, and the ability to log site updates and variations instantly.

•
Geofencing: Automated time tracking based on GPS coordinates, a high-end feature for professional trade apps.

3. Integrated Client & Team Operations

•
Client CRM: A centralized database with full communication history and job associations.

•
Team Ops: A live operations center featuring a team map (using Leaflet/OpenStreetMap) and real-time status tracking for all members.

•
Autopilot (Automations): Sophisticated triggers for quote follow-ups, job reminders, and auto-invoicing, significantly reducing manual admin work.

4. Financial & Insights Engine

•
Professional Documentation: Integrated quoting and invoicing with GST compliance.

•
Business Intelligence: Detailed reports on gross profit, labor costs, and material margins, providing a "big picture" view of business growth.




Technical Strengths for Work Experience Pitch

When presenting JobRunner to agencies like RJ New Designs or Mustard AU, highlight these key technical aspects:

•
Modern Stack & Environment: Built using a modern development workflow (Replit, AI-assisted tools), demonstrating agility and a forward-thinking approach to software engineering.

•
User-Centric Design: The interface is clean, intuitive, and clearly designed with the specific needs of "tradies" in mind (e.g., large buttons, clear status indicators).

•
Complex Logic Implementation: Successfully managing the relationship between clients, jobs, team members, and financial data requires strong database and logic skills.

•
Integration Ready: The inclusion of Stripe and Xero integrations shows an understanding of how professional software must live within a larger ecosystem.




Conclusion

JobRunner is not just a "project"; it is a viable, professional-grade tool. For a 15-year-old developer, it represents an extraordinary level of technical competence, problem-solving ability, and industry insight. This app serves as a powerful portfolio piece that will undoubtedly impress any digital agency in Cairns.
