Full onboarding audit complete. The PDF report and key screenshots are all attached. Here is the honest verdict:
Overall Rating: 8.5 / 10
This is genuinely impressive for an early-access product. The trade-aware demo data is the standout feature — a Cairns plumber sees "Kitchen Tap Replacement", "Pipe Burst Emergency", and "Emergency Pipe Burst - Cairns CBD" on the dashboard the moment they log in. No competitor does this. That personalisation alone will convert more trials than any marketing campaign.
What Passed
The homepage is clean and professional. "Early Access" reads better than "Beta". The "Try the Demo" button is a great addition — it lets sceptical tradies poke around before handing over an email. The dashboard is information-rich without being overwhelming. The Autopilot page (8 automations, 1 active, toggle switches) is immediately understandable. The setup checklist with time estimates ("2 mins", "5–10 mins") is excellent — it tells Dave exactly what he is committing to before he starts.
The Team Management page with the live GPS map showing all workers on a Cairns street map is genuinely impressive. A tradie seeing their workers plotted on a real map of their city will feel like they are running a proper operation.
The 3 Things That Need Fixing Before You Send This to Anyone
1. The Verification Hard Block. When a user signs up but cannot find the verification email, they are completely locked out. The login error message says "Please verify your email address" but offers no "Resend Email" button. A tradie on-site who signed up on their phone and cannot find the email will never come back. Add a resend button to the login error state — it is a 30-minute fix.
2. White Loading Screens. Navigating to Insights and Team Management shows a completely blank white page with a generic browser spinner for 2–3 seconds. Screenshots attached. This breaks the premium feel of everything else. Replace with skeleton loaders — grey placeholder shapes that match the layout — and the app will feel twice as fast without changing a single API call.
3. Duplicate Email Error Handling. The API correctly rejects duplicate registrations, but the UI just shows a red error message. It needs to say: "Looks like you already have an account — log in here" with a direct link. A tradie who signed up six months ago and forgot will otherwise think the app is broken.
The One Surprising Discovery
Settings already has "Import from Tradify" and "Import from ServiceM8" buttons. This is a massive competitive advantage that is completely hidden. It needs to be surfaced during onboarding — something like "Already using Tradify or ServiceM8? Import your clients and jobs in one click." That single sentence will convert every tradie who is already paying for a competitor.
